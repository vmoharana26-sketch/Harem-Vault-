HAREM Vault - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may process Discord User IDs, Server IDs, card collections, inventories, virtual currency and game progress.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate card collection features, save progress, improve the bot and prevent abuse.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and service providers needed to operate the bot.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information.

5. Data Deletion

Users may contact the HAREM Vault team to request deletion of their stored data.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when necessary.

7. Contact

Contact the HAREM Vault team through its official Discord support server.
